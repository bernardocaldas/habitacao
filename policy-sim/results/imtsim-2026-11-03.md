# imtsim, first run on the real data (3 November 2026)

Run on Bernardo's Mac: `uv run imtsim … --housing-dir estimador-data-housing/data/housing --tax
estimador-data/data/housing/municipal_tax/municipal_tax.csv --year 2025 --draws 4000`
(estimador-data `collect-data municipal-tax`: DGAL cash collected 2025, AT rate for tax year 2024).
Prices and rents: INE 2025 (2 prices and 53 rents imputed). This run left out the 8 municípios
with no rate communicated for tax year 2024 (Espinho, São João da Madeira, Mira, Lagos,
Gondomar, Ferreira do Zêzere, Porto Santo, Santa Cruz das Flores); `data.py` now gives them the
0.3% legal minimum, so the next run covers all 308. The full JSON and per-município CSV stay on
the Mac (`~/code/habitacao-imtsim/policy-sim/results/imtsim-2026-11-03/`).

**Prior predictive, no calibration: medians with 90% intervals. Prototype, not for publication.**

Census 2021: 5,970,677 classic family dwellings; 375,118 vacant for other reasons (82,390 in
buildings from before 1946), 348,097 vacant for sale or rent.
